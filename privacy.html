Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026 (now covers Android)

Doodle Alive is a drawing game for young children. It is built to collect nothing. This policy covers the app on iPhone and iPad (App Store) and on Android phones and tablets (Google Play).

What we collect

Nothing about your child or you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no chat. Drawings, stickers, stars and settings are stored only on this device, in the app's own storage, and are deleted when the app is deleted.

Subscriptions

If a grown-up buys a subscription, the store handles the payment: Apple (App Store) on iPhone and iPad, Google (Google Play) on Android. We never see card details. We use RevenueCat to check which worlds a subscription unlocks. RevenueCat receives the store's purchase receipt and a random, anonymous app user id made on this device. It never receives a name, email, photo or drawing. See RevenueCat's privacy policy for how they handle receipts. If no purchase feature is used, nothing is sent.

Photos

A grown-up can save a sticker to the device's photos (behind the grown-up gate). On iPhone and iPad the app asks only for permission to add photos. On Android it saves the picture to the gallery; older Android versions (9 and earlier) ask for storage permission to do this. The app never reads, browses or uploads your photos.

Microphone, camera, location, contacts

Not used.

Children

Doodle Alive is made for children under 13 and follows COPPA, GDPR-K, Apple's Kids Category rules and Google Play's Families Policy: no personal information is collected, so there is nothing to review, export or delete. Grown-ups can remove all data by deleting the app. Purchases, settings and links that leave the app sit behind a grown-up gate.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
