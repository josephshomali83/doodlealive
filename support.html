Grown-up corner

Settings, subscriptions and saving pictures are behind the grown-up gate: hold the lock button for 3 seconds, then type the number shown in words (for example sixty-nine → 69).

Subscriptions

The first 3 friends in every world are free forever. A subscription unlocks the rest. You only ever have one subscription: changing plan simply upgrades or downgrades it.

Cancel or change plan

On iPhone or iPad: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Doodle Alive.

On Android: Google Play → your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Doodle Alive.

Or in the app on either: grown-up corner → Subscription → Manage subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.

Restore purchases

New tablet or phone, or reinstalled the app? Sign in with the same Apple or Google account, then open the grown-up corner → Subscription → Restore purchases. If you had a 1- or 3-world plan, pick your worlds again by tapping them.

Family Sharing

Subscriptions work with Apple Family Sharing and Google Play Family Library, so one subscription covers your family's devices on the same store.

Refunds

Payments are handled by the store, so refunds come from them. Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com. Google Play: request a refund on Google Play.

My child's drawings disappeared

Drawings are stored only on the device. Deleting the app deletes them. To keep a favourite, save it to Photos (iPhone, iPad) or the gallery (Android) from the sticker book (grown-up corner).

Play time

In the grown-up corner you can set a play-time limit. When it ends, the friend yawns and goes to sleep, so there's no surprise "game over".
