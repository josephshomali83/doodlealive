For 2–6 year olds

Your child picks a friend (a T. rex, an octopus, a fire truck, a toucan, over 260 of them, always with their real names), traces it along friendly dashed lines, and paints it any way they like. Then the painting wakes up: it blinks, giggles when tapped, and goes on a little adventure to find its favourite food.

Made for little hands

  • No fail states, no timers, no enemies.
  • Nothing to read: everything is spoken or shown.
  • The tracing band gets narrower as your child gets better.
  • No ads, no accounts, no tracking. Works offline.
  • For iPad, iPhone and Android tablets and phones.
